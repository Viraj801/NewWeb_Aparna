Who We Are

A Legacy of Quality in the Heart of Basmati Country

Located at Bareli, Dist. Raisen (M.P.) — the core of Pusa Basmati rice agricultural land.

🚁 Aerial view of our Bareli facility — nestled in lush paddy farmland

Manufacturer & Exporter of All Basmati Rice Variants

Established in 2011, Aparna Foods Pvt Ltd is engaged in milling, processing, customizable packaging and supply of the finest qualities of Basmati Rice in the form of Raw, Steam and Sella to markets worldwide.

Our export business has grown strongly over the past years — we supply Basmati Rice to the Middle East, Europe and Saudi Arabia, with private-label packaging options from 5 kg to 50 kg.

Quality Journey

From Paddy to Packing — Our Process

Every step of our operation is carefully monitored to deliver premium quality Basmati Rice.

1

Procurement & Testing

We procure paddy directly at our warehouses, where it undergoes strict physical and chemical testing to ensure it meets our quality standards.

2

Automated Milling

Our milling process is carried out using imported, fully automated machinery, ensuring precision, consistency and superior quality at 200 MT per day.

3

Hygiene & Safety

We fumigate our milling section regularly and adhere to stringent hygiene and food safety standards right up to the final packing and stuffing stage.

4

Customizable Export Packaging

Fully customizable packaging (5 kg – 50 kg) with private-label options, so clients can promote their own brands with confidence.
